## New

### Both platforms

- **Water at a glance.** The water card is fresher and more compact. Every glass counts, even past your goal, and you log one or two glasses with a single tap.
- **Activity, clearly explained.** The activity card shows first how many calories your activity adds to today's goal.

### iOS

- **A new design on Apple Watch.** The watch app is calmer and more modern. You set your weight with the Digital Crown, and Intake fits on every watch face, including a Smart Stack card that logs a glass of water with one tap.

## Bug fixes

### Both platforms

- **Stats add up.** Calories you move with the weekly budget count once in Stats, days without entries no longer count as a deficit, and your weight history starts at your start date.
- **Calories from the other days.** Even if your daily goal is below your basal metabolic rate, you can take calories from the other days for today.
- **Your own Claude key.** With your own Claude key, Intake AI reliably returns a result, with Haiku too.

### iOS

- **Your macro goals stay.** Custom macro goals stay as you set them on all your devices.
- **Edit, then log.** Correct the values of a scanned product and you can log it right away.
- **Sodium in Apple Health.** Sodium reaches Apple Health with the right value, also for foods that only list salt.

### Android

- **Every glass counts.** The water glass count keeps going after you reach your goal, in the app and in the widget.
- **Send a submission again.** A submission that couldn't be sent opens with a tap, so you can check it and send it again.
