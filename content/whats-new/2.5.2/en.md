## The tab bar works again on the iOS 27 beta

On the iOS 27 beta, three tabs simply stopped reacting: the barcode scanner, Recipes, and Create. Tapping them did nothing at all, which made scanning a product or building a recipe impossible on that beta. The tabs are tappable again, so everything in the tab bar behaves the way it does on iOS 26.

If you are on the iOS 27 beta, this update is worth installing right away. On iOS 26 and earlier nothing changes, since the problem never occurred there.

## iOS only

This release ships for iOS only. The bug is specific to the iOS 27 beta, so there is no matching Android update — your Android version stays on 2.5.1 and is not missing anything.

You can find the full changelog [here](https://featurevoting.tobibechtold.dev/app/intake/changelog).

Thank you for using Intake. I hope you enjoy the new release.
