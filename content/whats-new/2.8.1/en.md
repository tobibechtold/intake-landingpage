## Bug fixes

### iOS

- **Zero-calorie foods.** Foods and drinks without calories, like water or a diet soda, now log reliably, also when you scan their barcode.

### Android

- **Scanning keeps you in the meal.** After you add a scanned product, you stay in that meal, ready for the next item, instead of landing back on Today.

Coming straight from an older version? Everything new in Intake 2.8, from the weekly budget to the month calendar, is [here](/en/whats-new/2.8.0).
