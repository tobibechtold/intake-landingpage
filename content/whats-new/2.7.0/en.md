## Siri logs for you

Say "Log 100 g oats in Intake" and the oats are in your diary without opening the app. "Log water in Intake", "Log my weight in Intake" and "How many calories do I have left in Intake" work the same way. Start and end your fast just as easily.

**Your foods, your device.** Siri searches your own foods: favourites, own products and what you logged recently. Everything runs on the iPhone; nothing goes to a server. Leave out the amount and Intake takes 100 g. Leave out the meal and the food lands in the one that fits the time of day. Water without an amount is 250 ml.

**A weight in your unit.** Say "74.5 kilos" or "164 pounds" and Intake logs the weight and reads it back in the unit you used.

**In Shortcuts too.** All six actions sit in the Shortcuts app under Intake and can go into your own flows, for instance reading the morning weight off the scale and logging it. The Siri phrases come in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Dutch and Portuguese.

## Your foods in Spotlight

Pull down on the home screen and type the name of a food you have logged before. The result opens Intake straight on the serving screen. Intake hands your library to Spotlight after launch and after every entry, at most once a minute, and only on your device.

On iOS 27 the same library also answers the system's search: Siri can search and open your foods, and when you point the camera at a dish with Visual Intelligence, Intake suggests matching foods from your library.

## Swipe to remove

Rows in favourites, recents and library go with a swipe to the left on iOS 27. The long press with its context menu stays, on iOS 26 as well.

## Ingredients in your order

Tap Edit in a recipe's ingredients and drag them into place. The order is kept in the recipe view and when logging.

## Built for iOS 27

The soft fade under every bar stays soft instead of turning into a hard edge on iOS 27. Layouts adapt to wider screens: more tiles per row on an iPad, long text at a readable width, and date pickers open at a medium height instead of following the screen height. Intake still runs on iOS 26.

## Bug fixes

### iOS

- With Display Zoom or large text on a smaller iPhone, a logged food could not be changed or deleted: the buttons in the bar had collapsed into a menu that did nothing. Delete, More and Save are now labelled and stay visible.
- The bar on the Today page shows a glyph with a label instead of the translated "Today" button, which did not fit the bar in some languages.
