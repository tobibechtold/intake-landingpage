## Faster help by email

When you write to us from Contact in Settings, Intake adds your app version, system version, device and language below your message. That way we know right away what you are using and can help you faster. You see everything in the mail before you send it.

## New on Android: remove from Frequent and Recent

Long-press a food in Frequent or Recent, or swipe it left, to remove it.

## Bug fixes

### iOS

- **Delete and edit at any time.** You can delete and edit entries and whole meals at any time, also on days you planned ahead.
