You can change what Intake may access later in Apple Health or Health Connect. See [What Intake reads and writes](/en/help/health-read-write) for the details.

On iPhone, new Apple Health data also arrives in the background. “Renew Apple Health Permissions” asks again after an update, for example for new nutrients. On Android it is “Renew Health Connect permissions”.
