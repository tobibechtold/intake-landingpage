What Intake asks, and why:

- **Your goal:** “Lose weight”, “Maintain weight” or “Gain weight”. It decides whether your daily target sits below or above what you burn, see [Calorie goal](/en/help/calorie-goal).
- **Apple Health or Health Connect:** If you connect it, Intake fills in your body data, and later your steps and workouts count too. You can skip this, see [Connect your health app](/en/help/connect-health).
- **About you:** Your sex, birthday, height, weight and activity level. Intake works out what you burn from them, see [Body data](/en/help/body-data).
- **Your daily target:** At the end, Intake shows the result. Tap “Start tracking” to begin.
