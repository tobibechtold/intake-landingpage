## What you can say to Siri

- “Log water in Intake”: logs 250 ml.
- “Log my weight in Intake”: Siri asks for your weight.
- “Log food in Intake”: Siri asks which food and how many grams. It goes into the meal that fits the time of day.
- “How many calories do I have left in Intake”: Siri tells you what’s left for today.
- “Start a fast in Intake” and “End my fast in Intake”: when fasting is turned on.

Say the phrases exactly like this. Siri can’t pick up an amount in the same sentence, so give it when Siri asks. Siri knows the foods that are already in Intake.

## Shortcuts

In the Shortcuts app, you’ll find every action under Intake. There you can set a fixed amount and meal, for example for a shortcut that logs 500 ml of water every morning.
