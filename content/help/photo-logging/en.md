You can add a note to the photo, like “half portion” or “no sauce”. It works for plates and for packaged products. Each estimate takes one photo.

The photo becomes the photo of your entry. If the estimate is off, reply in the chat or see [Correct an Intake AI estimate](/en/help/correct-ai-estimate).
