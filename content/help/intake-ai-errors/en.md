## Common messages

- **Key rejected:** copy the key from your provider again and save it in Settings under “Intake AI”.
- **Rate limit reached:** your key sent too many requests. Free keys only allow a few per minute, so wait a moment.
- **Provider busy:** try again in a moment.
- **Model not available:** pick a different model in Settings. For some errors, Intake suggests another model itself.
- **Request too large:** use a smaller photo or a shorter message.
- **No key saved:** add your key in Settings.
- **Subscription not active:** check the subscription in Settings under “Intake AI” or restore it.

On iPhone, “Retry” below the message sends the same request again; on Android, just send your message once more. If none of this helps, write to us via “Contact” in Settings and include the message.
