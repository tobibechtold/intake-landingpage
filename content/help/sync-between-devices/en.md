## iPhone and iPad

With [iCloud sync](/en/help/icloud-sync), Intake syncs your profile and goals, your diary, library, recipes, your own products, body measurements and your fasting schedule, and photos if you like.

Data from Apple Health, such as steps, workouts or a weight from your scale, doesn’t go through iCloud. Each device reads it from Apple Health itself. Reminders only apply on the device where you set them up.

## Android

Intake on Android doesn’t sync between devices. With the [encrypted Google Drive backup](/en/help/google-drive-backup), you keep a copy of your data and can bring it to a new device.
