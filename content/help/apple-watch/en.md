## Install it

On your iPhone, open the Watch app, scroll down under “My Watch” to “Available Apps” and tap “Install” next to Intake.

## What the Watch app does

Swipe between three pages:

- **Overview:** calories left in the ring, with protein, carbs and fat, water and weight below.
- **Water:** plus and minus count one glass of your glass size, “Custom” logs an amount of your own.
- **Weight:** plus and minus change your weight in steps of 0.1 kg. Intake saves shortly after your last tap.

The Watch shows water in ml and weight in kg.

## Complications

While editing a watch face, you’ll find “Calories Left”, “Calories Eaten” and “Water Progress” under Intake.

There’s also a guide in Intake under Settings > “Appearance” > “Apple Watch App”.
