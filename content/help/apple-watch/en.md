## Install it

On your iPhone, open the Watch app, scroll down under “My Watch” to “Available Apps” and tap “Install” next to Intake.

## What the Watch app does

Swipe between three pages:

- **Overview:** calories left in the ring, with protein, carbs and fat below.
- **Water:** glasses and millilitres with a bar that keeps counting past your goal. The plus logs one glass of your glass size, the minus takes one away, and “Custom” at the top logs an amount of your own.
- **Weight:** turn the Digital Crown or tap plus and minus to change your weight in steps of 0.1 kg, then tap “Save”. Below it you see the change since your last weigh-in.

The Watch shows water in ml and weight in kg.

## Complications

While editing a watch face, you’ll find “Calories Left”, “Calories Eaten” and “Water Progress” under Intake, as a circle, a rectangle, in a corner and as a line of text.

In the Smart Stack, the “Intake” card shows your calories left and your water in the morning, at noon and in the evening. Its button logs a glass without opening the app.

There’s also a guide in Intake under Settings > “Appearance” > “Apple Watch App”.
