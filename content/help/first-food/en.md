If you only know the calories, leave the search field empty, tap “Create” and choose “Quick add only”, see [Quick-add calories](/en/help/quick-add-calories).

You log the amount in portions or as a custom amount, see [Portions and units](/en/help/portions-units). Foods you’ve eaten before are quicker to find next time, see [Recent foods and favorites](/en/help/recent-frequent-favorites).
