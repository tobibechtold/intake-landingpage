title: "Get more out of Intake"
short: "Once logging feels easy, these features take you further: recipes and routines, Intake AI, fasting, stats, widgets and sync."
---

- **Recipes and routines:** Set up a dish once, then log it again quickly, see [Create a recipe](/en/help/create-recipe) and [Routines](/en/help/routines).
- **Intake AI:** Describe or photograph your food, and Intake estimates calories and macros, see [Set up Intake AI](/en/help/set-up-intake-ai). By subscription or with [your own API key](/en/help/own-api-key).
- **Fasting:** Set an eating window and see how long your fast still runs, see [Set up fasting](/en/help/set-up-fasting).
- **Stats:** Calories, macros, weight and water by week, month and year, see [Stats at a glance](/en/help/stats).
- **Widgets, Watch and Shortcuts:** Intake on your Home Screen (see [Widgets](/en/help/widgets)), and on iPhone also on [Apple Watch](/en/help/apple-watch) and with [Siri and Shortcuts](/en/help/siri-shortcuts).
- **Sync and backup:** iCloud sync on iPhone, an encrypted backup in Google Drive on Android, see [Sync between devices](/en/help/sync-between-devices).
