From then on, Intake syncs on its own whenever you change something or open the app. Pull down on Today to fetch changes right away. Anything you log through Siri, widgets or the Apple Watch comes along too.

With “Sync photos” off, new photos stay on this device. Data from Apple Health, such as steps, doesn’t go through iCloud, see [What syncs between devices](/en/help/sync-between-devices).

After the update to the new iCloud sync, Intake shows “iCloud sync is better now” once and syncs your data again. You can keep using the app meanwhile.
