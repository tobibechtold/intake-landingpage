With “Order in Health Connect”, the order you set in Health Connect under “Data sources and priority” decides. Apps missing there don’t count.

If a line is marked red, tap it. Intake then explains what is missing, see [Fix Health Connect problems](/en/help/health-connect-troubleshooting).
