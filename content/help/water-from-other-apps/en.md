  - text: "Under “Read from Apple Health”, leave “Water from other apps” on. It is on from the start."
    ios:
      image: ./assets/en/ios/2.webp
  - text: "On the “Water” card this water is listed under “From Apple Health”, with the app it came from."
    ios:
      image: ./assets/en/ios/3.webp

Intake never writes this water back to Apple Health, so nothing is counted twice. The arrow next to “From Apple Health” reads it again right away.
