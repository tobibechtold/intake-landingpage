## Add a control

Open Control Center, tap the plus at the top left and then “Add a Control”. Search for Intake and pick one.

On the Lock Screen, swap one of the two buttons at the bottom for an Intake control while customizing it.

## The controls

- **Search:** opens the food search.
- **Scan:** opens the barcode scanner.
- **Quick:** opens “Create” to log something without searching.
- **Ask Intake AI:** opens the Intake AI chat.
- **AI Camera:** opens Intake AI with the camera.

The two Intake AI controls need the Intake AI subscription or your own API key.
