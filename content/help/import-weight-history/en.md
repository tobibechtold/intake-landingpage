“Only fill missing days” leaves every day that already has a weight as it is. “Overwrite existing entries” also replaces weights you logged yourself with the Apple Health value. Intake then tells you how many days it took over.

If steps or activity from the last few weeks are missing, “Refresh last month from Apple Health” right above it reads the last 31 days again.
